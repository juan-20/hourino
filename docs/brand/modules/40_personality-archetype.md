# 40 — Personality & Archetype (scoped: logo mark)

Scoped session: only the questions needed to brief the logo/icon mark. Other
modules (purpose, positioning, audience, voice, narrative) were skipped and
are inferred from existing material (`packages/ui/DESIGN.md`, landing copy in
`apps/web/messages/en.json`).

## Raw

- Existing brief (DESIGN.md): "young and alternative, but simple and direct"
  — neobrutalism, riso print, terracotta, editorial sensibility, executed
  with restraint.
- Product promise (landing hero): "Where did your month go? Now you'll know."
  — "Drop your hours on a calendar, tag them, and see your month at a glance."
- Q: One idea the mark should make people feel first? → **"Small, friendly
  hours"** (the `-ino` diminutive: a tiny, affectionate unit of time).
- Q: How much character? → **"Subtle face in geometry"** — a clock/hour form
  where hands or dots read as a tiny face only on second look; must work at
  16px and stay professional.
- Q: Feeling to stay away from? → **"Corporate timesheet"** (Toggl / Harvest /
  Clockify — stopwatch icons, billable-hours seriousness).

## Synthesis

Archetype: **Everyperson with a streak of Jester** — approachable, on your
side, lightly playful; never the Ruler/Sage authority of a timesheet tool.

Mark brief — candidate formulations:
1. "A little hour with a face you notice on the second glance."
2. "The clock, made small and kind — not a stopwatch."
3. "One chunky riso-printed hour, smiling quietly."

Constraints for concepting:
- Geometry first; the face is emergent (two dots + a hand/curve), not drawn on.
- Must survive 16px favicon: ≤ 3 shapes inside the container, thick strokes.
- Duotone only: espresso ink `#432F2E` + powder blue `#c3DAE8`, paper
  background; hard offset shadow as the only "depth".
- Avoid: stopwatch crowns/buttons, checkmarks, lightning, dollar/billing cues,
  thin-line corporate clock faces, bubbly mascot limbs.

Open questions:
- Wordmark treatment (lowercase `hourino` in Space Grotesk is the default
  assumption — lowercase matches the product copy's "hourino").
- Whether the face should "animate" (blink) in-product — deferred.
